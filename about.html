ABOUT LEGRAND_MIRTH

legrand_mirth is a sample label for producers. Sample packs, loops, one-shots, drum kits, textures and sounds made to be sampled, flipped, chopped and turned into something new.

THE SOUNDS

The library is built around sounds that can become part of your own productions rather than simply filling space.

Everything is designed to be manipulated, processed and transformed into something that sounds like you.

FREE & SHOP

Some sounds are available for free, while other releases are available through the shop. All sounds are provided under the legrand_mirth License.

STREAMING

Sounds are royalty-free for use in individual tracks up to 1,000,000 cumulative streams.

If a track exceeds 1,000,000 cumulative streams, no royalties or payments are required. The only additional requirement is that legrand_mirth must receive the credit described in the License.
